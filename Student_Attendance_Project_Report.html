CHAPTER 1: INTRODUCTION

In secondary schools, attendance recording is a foundational routine that directly influences academic continuity, school discipline, and examination eligibility. Under CBSE Regulation Rule 13, maintaining a minimum of 75% attendance is strictly enforced for Class 10 board examination candidates.

EduTrack Pro is a complete full-stack web application developed to replace manual paper registers with an automated, instantaneous roll-call system featuring live turnout calculations, CBSE 75% Defaulter Radar, and spreadsheet integration.

CHAPTER 7: FORM CREATION & ATTENDANCE REGISTER

The system features intuitive input forms that allow single-click attendance marking. Teachers can mark an entire class as Present in 1 second, and toggle absentees with individual chips (Present, Absent, Late, Half-Day).

CHAPTER 3 & 8: DEFAULTER RADAR (<75%) & REPORTS

The Defaulter Radar automatically computes cumulative attendance and flags students below 75%. It calculates the exact number of consecutive attended days needed to regain eligibility and generates official parent notices.
